And as dawn breaks
The sun rises in all it's glory
Shining over us sinners
They're ready, us be mourned
Henceforward, we shall be dead
And lo, behold their pointed guns
I look at the lights, bright a-reflect of sun
One last time, last time to witness

The shadows and the city
Whole life, I've never found any-like
In a blink of an eye, beauty ever fleeting
Tears of joy fill my eyes, for it's everything
Nothing more to witness, all is done
Time to die
