Forgive me,
Said the odd paper
that you read with the two of your eyes:
Your beautiful eyes.

Not from your ex,
not your friends' gift
And neither the apology of your dearest enemy,
but from someone;
from the person who waited too long,
long of a time 'til time interferes himself:
he cut us apart to different sides of fate,
long chains of fate that ravels all the way
to the unknown.

Forgive me,
who stated my love at ends
throwing sparks into your hands
while I flee, leaving you with your friends
can I ever make amends
to things that time rends?
