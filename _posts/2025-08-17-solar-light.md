Crushed under the solar light
Could I possibly put a fight?
Never once was a fleeting night
Where I could rest tight
Without the sun's scorching might

And I wish I could fight for you
But you disappeared
And you brought the night along
Leaving me in solitude, I-
I wish you were here
with me
