title: "Now, After the Shoal"

she shoals before she moors
she hears before she listens
she shovels before she digs

merely upon the going thither;
of going over yonder,
hither a mistral to overcome
to be struck under:
the soulfully hopeless
and hopefully soulless

whither shall ye gone?
gold over the hills,
gold at the end of.

over the hills:
overcome.

καὶ ὁ Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν αὐτῷ· Ὕπαγε, ἡ πίστις σου σέσωκέν σε. καὶ εὐθὺς ἀνέβλεψεν, καὶ ἠκολούθει αὐτῷ ἐν τῇ ὁδῷ.
